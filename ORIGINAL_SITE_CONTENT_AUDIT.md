## Claims deliberately not promoted

- `700+ Members`, `7+ Countries`, `10+ Opportunities Posted`, and `Commission Earned` appear on the old homepage but have no supporting data in the supplied source material.
- The original site describes an app and public opportunity platform as coming soon. It does not support claims that either is already live.
- The current repository labels its opportunity, event, case-study and magazine entries as **EXAMPLE listings** in `src/seed/index.ts`. They are not original-site records and must remain hidden until AGBN supplies and approves the real content.
- The old About page’s broad `54 countries` statement is preserved as a brand ambition only, not a live network-size claim.
